Turon Recipe

Description

Classic Filipino leche flan requires just a few basic pantry ingredients for both the rich custard and the sweet caramel topping.

Ingredients

    Caramel Topping

  • 1 cup granulated white sugar
  • 2 to 4 tablespoons water (optional, to help melt the sugar evenly)

    Custard Flan Base

  • 10 large egg yolks (some recipes use up to 12 or add 2 whole eggs for a firmer texture)
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup evaporated milk (or a standard 360/370ml can)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • A squeeze of calamansi or lemon juice (optional, to cut the eggy taste)
  • A pinch of salt (optional)

Steps

  1. Make the caramel: Place your sugar in a round metal pan (llanera) directly over low heat on the stove. Stir gently until the sugar melts completely and turns a golden brown color, then set it aside to cool and harden.

  2. Mix the custard base: In a large bowl, combine your egg yolks, sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, and vanilla extract. Use a fork or a whisk to mix everything together very gently in one direction to avoid creating air bubbles.

  3. Strain the mixture: Pour the liquid mixture through a fine-mesh strainer or cheesecloth into another container to remove any remaining egg lumps or small bubbles.

  4. Assemble the pan: Pour the strained custard mixture slowly into your llanera right on top of the hardened caramel layer.

  5. Cover the pan: Seal the top of your llanera tightly using a sheet of aluminum foil to prevent water from dripping into the custard while steaming.

  6. Steam the flan: Place the covered pan inside a steamer or a large pot with a steaming rack over simmering water. Steam on low to medium heat for 30 to 45 minutes until the flan is firm but still slightly jiggly in the center.

  7. Cool and chill: Remove the pan from the steamer, let it cool completely at room temperature, and then place it in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours before running a knife around the edges and flipping it onto a serving plate.

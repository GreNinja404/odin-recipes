Easy To Make Filipino Dishes

Why Filipino Food:

One of the reasons why you should try filipino food is because,
Filipino cooking uses simple preparation methods like frying,
roasting, or boiling,but transforms dishes with powerful flavor combinations.

Is it that good?:

Yes, Filipino food is that good.
But it depends a lot on what you order and your personal taste preferences.
Filipino cuisine ranked 25th in the world according to the TasteAtlas Awards.
